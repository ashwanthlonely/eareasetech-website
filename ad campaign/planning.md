Ashwanth, the problem is probably **not that ₹15,000 is too expensive**. The bigger problem is that your current ads are generating **people who are interested in “Data Science” but are not sufficiently motivated to buy a ₹15K program**.

Your observation — *“I clicked the ad by mistake”* — is a classic sign of **low-intent lead generation**. Meta can generate lots of cheap leads, but cheap leads are not necessarily qualified leads. Meta itself recommends optimizing toward **conversion/quality leads** rather than simply maximizing lead volume. ([Facebook][1])

For an online course around ₹15K, current Indian education benchmarks put raw Meta leads roughly in the ₹100–₹350 range, depending on targeting, funnel and program. But the important metric is **cost per qualified/enrolled student**, not CPL. ([Sotros Infotech][2])

## What I would do for EarEase Nexus

Don't build the business around:

> Ad → Lead → Sales call → ₹15,000 payment

Build this instead:

> **Attention → Free value → Qualification → Trust → Counseling → Enrollment**

### 1. Stop selling the ₹15K course directly

Your advertisement shouldn't primarily say:

> "Data Science Course – ₹15,000"

Instead sell the **transformation**:

> **Become Job-Ready in Data Science**
>
> Build real ML + Deep Learning projects
> Learn Python → ML → DL → GenAI
> Build a portfolio
> Get mentor guidance
> Limited seats

Then your landing page/form reveals:

**Program investment: ₹15,000 + GST**

This filters out people who genuinely cannot/will not pay.

However, don't hide the price completely. Put it clearly before the final counseling stage so your sales team isn't wasting hours.

# 11. Your ₹15K price may actually be an advantage

Don't immediately reduce it.

If you go:

₹15,000 → ₹9,999 → ₹5,999

you may get **more leads but worse students**.

Instead create payment flexibility.

For example:

**Option A**

₹15,000 + GST upfront

**Option B**

₹5,000 × 3 installments + GST

**Option C**

₹2,500/week × 6

Subject to your actual commercial/finance policies.

This addresses **cash-flow objections** without destroying your perceived value.
